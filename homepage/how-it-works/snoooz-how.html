How it works

Onboarded like a new hire. But Snoooz learns faster than any employee.

It starts by drafting under your review, and earns its way to autopilot.

1 Connect

Connect your inboxes.

Gmail, Outlook, or any IMAP/SMTP mailbox, including shared addresses like support@ and sales@. Nothing to migrate, nothing to rip out.

Train it on your knowledge.

3 Review

Start in review mode.

Snoooz drafts every reply for your team to approve. You see exactly how good it is before it sends a single email.

4 Autopilot

Switch on autopilot.

When you're ready, Snoooz replies, routes, and follows up on its own, and sends anything unusual, sensitive, or unfamiliar straight to a person.
