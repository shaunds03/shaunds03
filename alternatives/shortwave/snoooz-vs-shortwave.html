In practice

Same email. Two very different days.

Here is what actually happens when a real message lands, in each tool.

A pricing enquiry hits sales@

-In Shortwave

The filter labels it Sales and it lands in the right split. A rep opens it, reads it, asks the AI to draft a reply, checks the pricing themselves, sends it, then sets a reminder to follow up in three days.

About six minutes of a person's attention, and only after they get to it
✓In Snoooz

Snoooz reads the intent, pulls the current pricing from your approved docs, checks the sender against your CRM, sends the answer, routes the thread to the account owner, and schedules a follow-up that stops the moment they reply.

A few seconds, at 2am if that is when it arrived

An angry customer emails support@

-In Shortwave

The message gets bundled with everything else. Whoever is triaging spots the tone, comments to loop in a manager, and assigns it. It waits for someone to notice.

✓In Snoooz

Sentiment analysis flags it on arrival, Snoooz escalates it straight to the right person, and the customer gets an acknowledgement immediately rather than after the queue clears.

A repeat question lands in info@ for the ninetieth time

-In Shortwave

A snippet makes it faster to answer. Someone still answers it.

✓In Snoooz

Snoooz answers it from your knowledge base and sends. Nobody sees it unless they want to.

Half the team is on Outlook

-In Shortwave

✓In Snoooz

Same automations, same knowledge, same routing, across Gmail, Outlook, and IMAP.
